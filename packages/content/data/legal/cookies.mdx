# Cookie Policy

**Last updated: July 30, 2026**

This policy explains how SERP Software uses cookies and browser storage on
`serp.software`.

## Cookies and analytics

The site is delivered by Cloudflare, which may use strictly necessary cookies or
similar signals for security and reliable delivery. SERP Software is configured with
Google Tag Manager; tags enabled in that container may use cookies or collect usage
events according to their own configuration and policies.

Optional authentication features are currently disabled. If they are enabled later,
the authentication provider may set essential session and security cookies.

## Local storage

The application stores these preferences in your browser rather than in D1:

| Stored preference | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `llms-txt-hub-favorites` | Saved products |
| `websites-show-all` | Expanded directory preference |
| `websites-sort-by` | Directory sort preference |
| `category-sort-by` | Category sort preference |
| `recentSearches` | Recent autocomplete searches |

Local-storage values remain on your device until you clear browser data. Clearing them
removes saved favorites and resets search, sort, and display preferences.

## Managing your preferences

You can delete cookies and local storage in your browser's privacy settings. Blocking
strictly necessary storage may affect security or site operation. Blocking analytics
storage should not prevent directory browsing.

## Changes and questions

We may update this policy when hosting, analytics, authentication, or application
storage changes. The date above identifies the current version.

Any future change to how policy questions are handled will be published on this page.
