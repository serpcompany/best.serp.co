# Submit Workflow

Anyone can fill in the `/submit` form. Typing the website address reads the page, without AI,
and proposes the product name, a short description, and a logo (the site icon or the social
image); every proposal stays editable, and the category is always chosen by hand. A website
that is already listed, already submitted, or blocked as prohibited is refused before anything
is saved, with a way forward: claim the listing, open your own submission, or message us.

Saving needs an account. A visitor who is not signed in continues to a 6-digit email code; the
form stays in their browser meanwhile and comes back filled in after sign-in. Continuing then
saves a draft to their account. Nothing is reviewed or published yet.

Next, the submitter chooses how to get listed. The free listing asks them to add the "Featured
on SERP" badge to their site as a plain, followed link to their future listing page: its `rel`
must not include `nofollow`, `sponsored`, or `ugc`, and the page must not tell search engines
to skip its links with a robots `nofollow`. They copy a snippet, publish it, and ask us to
check. A check reads the page as a browser would (comments, scripts, and templates don't count)
and reports exactly what it found: the badge missing, a link that isn't followed, a page that
asks search engines not to follow links, a link to the wrong page, or a site that could not be
reached. Connection problems never use up one of the ten checks, and checks are limited to one
every 30 seconds. When the badge checks out, the submission enters the review queue, the
submitter gets a confirmation email, and the SERP team is alerted.

A draft that is never finished gets reminder emails and expires 30 days after it was first
saved, which frees its website for anyone to submit. Drafts and submissions are listed in the
submitter's account, where they can pick up where they left off.

Submissions live in Cloudflare D1. The pages are in `apps/web/src/app/submit/` and
`apps/web/src/components/submit/`, the server boundary in `apps/web/src/lib/submissions/`, and the
data rules in `apps/web/src/db/submissions.ts`.
